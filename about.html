庞森予 PANG SENYU PRINCIPAL / PRIVATE HOMES & VILLAS

A good space does not explain itself

I am Pang Senyu / principal of Weijie Design in Shanghai / I work on private homes and villas / over the years I have come to believe one thing / a good space does not explain itself / I am not drawn to houses that surprise you at the door / I want the house to settle / so that in the third or fifth year you notice the hour when a certain light crosses the wall

Before a project I stand on site for a while / where the light comes from / where the wind goes / where people naturally stay / these matter more than style

I take private homes and villas from plan to completion / plan material lighting and furniture / one person follows it through / budget first / drawings second / beauty after that / based in Shanghai / projects overseas
