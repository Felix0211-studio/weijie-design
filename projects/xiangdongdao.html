03 07
EAST ISLAND
SHANGHAI

Water brought into the room / the house given to the light

Morning light lands on the water first / then folds into the living room / the waterside edge is almost without partitions / fewer walls than windows / the movement of water becomes the background

Materials are wood stone paper and a little metal / wood left unlacquered / colour recedes with the water level and the seasons / sitting inside the house seems to breathe

We kept the plan long and low / circulation follows the water / the house does not perform / it listens
