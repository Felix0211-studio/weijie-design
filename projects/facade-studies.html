A calm face toward the water

The facade is organised around one central arch / a horizontal band of windows / a deep eave that holds the sun

Lime plaster terracotta and dark metal / the palette is kept to three materials / weather will do the rest

The exterior is not a backdrop / it sets the pace of arrival from the river road to the door
